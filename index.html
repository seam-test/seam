Privacy

Your board, sewn into your wardrobe.

Seam reads the clothes you save on Pinterest and helps you find similar pieces secondhand.

What it does

You connect a Pinterest board. Seam looks at the silhouettes, colours and pieces you keep saving, then searches secondhand marketplaces for matches in your size and budget.

How it uses Pinterest

  • Connects to the account owner’s Pinterest account through Pinterest’s official API.
  • Requests read-only access to boards and pins.
  • Never posts, edits or deletes anything on Pinterest.

Status

Seam is in development and is currently used only by its creator, to test the Pinterest integration on their own boards. It is not yet open to other users.
