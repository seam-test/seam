Privacy policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

Who this covers

Seam is a personal project in development. At this stage it is used only by its creator, on the creator’s own Pinterest account. No other people’s data is collected. If Seam is opened to other users, this policy will be updated first.

What Seam accesses

Through Pinterest’s official API, with the account owner’s permission, Seam reads:

  • Basic account information (such as username).
  • Board names and the pins saved on them, including pin images, titles, descriptions and links.

Access is read-only. Seam does not create, edit or delete anything on Pinterest.

How the data is used

The data is used only to understand the style of the clothing on the owner’s boards and to find similar items on secondhand marketplaces. It is not sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.

Storage and retention

Seam follows Pinterest’s Developer Guidelines on what Pinterest data may be stored. Access tokens are kept private and are not shared. Data is deleted on request.

Revoking access

You can disconnect Seam at any time from your Pinterest account settings, or by emailing the contact below. Disconnecting stops all further access to your Pinterest data.
